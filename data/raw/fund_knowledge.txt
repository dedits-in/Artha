MUTUAL FUND KNOWLEDGE BASE - INDIA

=== LARGE CAP FUNDS (Low-Medium Risk) ===
Suitable for: Conservative to moderate investors, 3-5 year horizon
Expected Returns: 10-12% CAGR
Examples: Mirae Asset Large Cap Fund, HDFC Top 100 Fund, ICICI Prudential Bluechip Fund
Expense Ratio: 0.5-1.5%
Risk Level: Moderate
Best for: Stable long-term wealth creation, retirement, education goals

=== MID CAP FUNDS (Medium-High Risk) ===
Suitable for: Aggressive investors, 5-7 year horizon
Expected Returns: 12-15% CAGR
Examples: HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund, Kotak Emerging Equity Fund, Axis Midcap Fund
Expense Ratio: 0.7-1.8%
Risk Level: High
Best for: Wealth creation, long-term goals above 5 years

=== SMALL CAP FUNDS (High Risk) ===
Suitable for: Very aggressive investors, 7+ year horizon
Expected Returns: 15-18% CAGR
Examples: SBI Small Cap Fund, Nippon India Small Cap Fund, Quant Small Cap Fund
Expense Ratio: 0.8-2.0%
Risk Level: Very High
Best for: High growth, long-term goals above 7 years

=== ELSS / TAX SAVING FUNDS ===
Suitable for: Investors wanting tax benefit under Section 80C
Lock-in Period: 3 years
Expected Returns: 12-14% CAGR
Examples: Axis ELSS Tax Saver Fund, Mirae Asset ELSS Fund, Parag Parikh ELSS Fund
Tax Benefit: Up to ₹1.5 lakh deduction under Section 80C
Risk Level: High (equity-oriented)
Best for: Tax saving + wealth creation

=== HYBRID / BALANCED ADVANTAGE FUNDS ===
Suitable for: Moderate risk investors, 3-5 year horizon
Expected Returns: 9-11% CAGR
Examples: ICICI Prudential Balanced Advantage Fund, HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund
Expense Ratio: 0.5-1.5%
Risk Level: Moderate
Best for: First-time investors, medium-term goals like car purchase, travel

=== DEBT FUNDS (Low Risk) ===
Suitable for: Conservative investors, short-term goals
Expected Returns: 6-8% CAGR
Examples: HDFC Short Term Debt Fund, ICICI Prudential Corporate Bond Fund
Risk Level: Low
Best for: Emergency fund, short-term goals under 3 years, capital preservation

=== SHORT TERM GOALS (1-3 years) ===
Recommended: Debt funds, liquid funds, short duration funds
Avoid: Small cap, mid cap (too volatile for short horizon)

=== MEDIUM TERM GOALS (3-5 years) ===
Recommended: Hybrid funds, large cap funds, flexi cap funds
Examples: Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund, HDFC Flexi Cap Fund

=== LONG TERM GOALS (5+ years) ===
Recommended: Mid cap, small cap, ELSS, flexi cap funds
Expected Returns: 12-18% CAGR depending on category

=== SIP BEST PRACTICES ===
- Start early to benefit from compounding
- Increase SIP by 10% every year (step-up SIP)
- Stay invested through market volatility
- Diversify across 3-4 fund categories
- Review portfolio every 6 months

=== GOAL BASED RECOMMENDATIONS ===
Car Purchase (3-5 years): Hybrid funds + Large cap (60:40)
Retirement (10+ years): Mid cap + Large cap + ELSS (50:30:20)
Child Education (10-15 years): Mid cap + Small cap + Large cap (40:30:30)
Tax Saving: ELSS funds (3 year lock-in)
Emergency Fund: Liquid funds or short-term debt funds
